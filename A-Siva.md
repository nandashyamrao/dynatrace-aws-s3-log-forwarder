5| 🌺 **Sivapuranam – Verse 1 (Paayiram)**
6| =======================================
7| 
8| ### **Tamil**
9| 
10| **தொல்லை இருபிறவி சூழும் தளை நீக்கி  
11| அல்லல் அறுத்து ஆனந்தம் ஆக்கியதே  
12| எல்லை மருவா நெறியளிக்கும்  
13| வாதவூர் எங்கோன் திருவாசகம் என்னும் தேன்**
14| 
15| * * *
16| 
17| 🌼 **Transliteration**
18| ======================
19| 
20| **tollai iru-piravi sūzhum thaḷai nīkkī  
21| allal arutthu ānandam ākkiyathē  
22| ellai maruvā neṟi yaḷikkum  
23| Vāthavūr engkōn Thiruvāchagam ennum thēn**
24|

27| 🌿 **Compound Breakdown (Big words split clearly)**
28| ===================================================
29| 
30| *   **தொல்லை** = தொல் (ancient/long-lasting) + இல (difficulty)
31| *   **இருபிறவி** = இரு (two / repeated) + பிறவி (births / rebirth cycle)
32| *   **சூழும்** = சூழ் (surround) + உம் (that which)
33| *   **தளை** = தடை / பந்தம் (bondage, obstacle)
34| *   **நீக்கி** = நீக்கு (remove) + இ (having removed)
35| *   **அல்லல்** = suffering, pain
36| *   **அறுத்து** = cut off, end
37| *   **ஆனந்தம்** = ānanda (bliss)
38| *   **ஆக்கியதே** = ஆக்கு (make) + இதே (this is what did)
39| *   **எல்லை** = limit, boundary
40| *   **மருவா** = not approaching / not touching / beyond reach
41| *   **நெறி** = path, way
42| *   **அளிக்கும்** = அளி (grant) + கும் (future tense)
43| *   **வாதவூர் எங்கோன்** = Lord (engaōn) of Vadavoor (the birthplace of the saint)
44| *   **திருவாசகம்** = Thiru + Vāchagam (sacred utterance / holy scripture)
45| *   **என்னும் தேன்** = called “honey”, meaning sweetest divine nectar

73| ✨ **Simple Translation**
74| ========================
75| 
76| “He removed the ancient bondage of countless births,  
77| cut away all my suffering,  
78| and transformed me into pure bliss.
79| 
80| He who shows the limitless, immaculate path —  
81| the Lord of Vadavoor —  
82| His Thiruvasagam is honey-like nectar.”
83| 
84| * * *
85| 
86| 🔱 **Deep Spiritual Interpretation**
87| ====================================
88| 
89| This verse is **Manikkavasagar’s declaration of transformation**:
90| 
91| ### 🌟 1. **Liberation from countless past births**
92| 
93| “தொல்லை இருபிறவி” means the heavy karmic chain of many lifetimes.  
94| Shiva’s grace breaks this prison.
95| 
96| ### 🌟 2. **End of suffering**
97| 
98| “Allal aruthu” — Shiva doesn’t merely reduce suffering;  
99| He _cuts it at the root_.
100| 
101| ### 🌟 3. **Entry into divine bliss**
102| 
103| “Ānandam ākkiththē” —  
104| Grace does not just liberate; it fills the heart with bliss.
105| 
106| ### 🌟 4. **A path beyond human limitations**
107| 
108| “எல்லை மருவா நெறி” suggests the Advaitic, pure path:  
109| the path where duality dissolves, where ego no longer exists.
110| 
111| ### 🌟 5. **The scripture itself is nectar**
112| 
113| “திருவாசகம் என்னும் தேன்” —  
114| This hymn is not literature;  
115| it is **ananda itself**,  
116| sweet like honey,  
117| melting ignorance and awakening devotion.
118| 
119| ### 🌟 6. **Transformation through hearing**
120| 
121| The saint implies:  
122| _Just by hearing or reciting Thiruvasagam,  
123| one’s karmic knots begin to melt.
